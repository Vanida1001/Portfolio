# 01 – Goals

- Show who I am, my school path, my projects and my activities clearly and professionally.
- Main targets: universities and schools in **Singapore** (e.g. NUS, NTU, SMU) and **France**. Not decided yet, so the site must work for any destination.
- Field of interest: **business and/or logistics** (not final). The site should show the qualities these programmes look for: maths, economics (SES), organisation, teamwork, projects I led myself.
- Be readable by admissions teams in both countries, so the site is fully **bilingual French / English**. French school terms are explained in English (see `sections/education.md`).
- Don't depend on any one admissions platform: the site is a standalone link I can add to any application.
- Be **easy to update**: adding a project, activity or award should mean editing data in `app.js`, never rewriting HTML.
- Work well on **phone, tablet and computer**.
